# Community conduct

Treat people respectfully. Discuss the work and evidence, not a contributor's identity or worth. Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, doxxing, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

Give actionable feedback, credit others' work, and respect that maintainers and contributors may have limited time. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive contributions and limit participation when needed to protect the community. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer through an available GitHub private contact channel; do not publish personal details in an issue.
